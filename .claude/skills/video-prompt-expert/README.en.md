# AI Video Prompt Expert / video-prompt-expert

---

## Overview

Write AI video prompts with a director’s mindset — transform vague visual descriptions into precise, executable photography instructions, and generate MP4 videos directly without switching tools.

**Core Value**

- **Instruction-style prompts**: Every word is an executable photography directive — no vague descriptions, AI accurately reproduces your visual vision
- **Write-and-shoot integration**: Generate HD video directly after completing prompts — from concept to final product in one flow
- **Diagnostic capability**: Built-in diagnostics for common issues — get fix suggestions when encountering character inconsistency, camera movement chaos, and more

**Intended Users**

- 🎬 **Short video creators** — Professional prompts elevate video quality and reduce trial-and-error
- 📱 **Content creators / social media operators** — Quickly produce multi-scenario video content
- 🎨 **Creative professionals** — Transform abstract ideas into precise generation instructions

## Features

### Core Capabilities

- **Structured prompt writing**: Build prompts layer by layer following professional technical parameters to ensure completeness and executability

- **Common issue diagnostics**: Get direct fix suggestions for character inconsistency, camera movement chaos, poor image quality, and more
